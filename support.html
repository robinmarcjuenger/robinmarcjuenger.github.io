Common questions

Where is my data?

On your Mac, and nowhere else. None of these apps upload anything or need an account. See the privacy page.

Do I pay again for updates?

No. These are one-time purchases with no subscription.

Loopetize, my loop does not repeat in the game

Loop points travel as tags inside the file, and not every format can carry them. OGG and M4A carry LOOPSTART and LOOPLENGTH, which is what RPG Maker reads. WAV carries a sampler chunk instead, which Unity and audio middleware read. AIFF, FLAC, ALAC, and CAF cannot carry loop points at all. For a desktop RPG Maker build, export OGG.

Loopetize, the recording is much quieter than the music

That is expected. Microphones record with headroom, while finished music is normalised close to full scale. Select the recorded clip, open Volume, and press Normalize.

The app says it cannot be opened because the developer cannot be verified

This appears on builds that are not yet notarised. Right click the app, choose Open, and confirm once.

Which macOS do I need?

macOS 13 Ventura or later.

Reporting a bug

What helps most: what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. If the app quit, the crash report from Console under User Reports is very useful. Screenshots are welcome.
