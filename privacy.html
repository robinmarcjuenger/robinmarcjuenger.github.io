Privacy

Applies to Loopetize, Pocketize, Pocketize Video, Rememberize, and this website. Last updated 18 September 2026.

The short version. Your files stay on your Mac, unless you switch on one clearly marked setting in Rememberize. There is no account, no analytics, and no tracking.

What the apps collect about you

Nothing. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking, no advertising, and no user account. Your documents, audio, video, and notes are never uploaded, and no server belonging to RMJ4PLAY receives anything from the apps.

Where your files live

All processing happens locally on your device. Files are read from and written to locations you choose. Rememberize stores notes as plain Markdown files in a folder you pick, so they stay readable without the app.

When an app uses the network

Rememberize transcribes voice notes on your device. It offers two engines and a setting that controls whether anything may leave the Mac.

Parakeet, the default

The speech model is downloaded once from the model provider, the first time you use transcription. That download contains no information about you. Afterwards transcription runs entirely on your Mac and needs no internet.

Apple speech recognition, optional

If you choose Apple's engine instead, recognition is requested as on device only, so the audio stays on your Mac. There is a separate setting, switched off by default, that allows Apple's servers to be used. Only if you turn that setting on, and only for languages Apple cannot handle on device, is audio sent to Apple, where it is processed under Apple's privacy policy.

The other apps

Loopetize, Pocketize, and Pocketize Video do not use the network at all.

System permissions

Loopetize

Microphone access is used to record audio into your project. Screen recording permission is used only to capture system audio, because that is the mechanism macOS provides for it. No image of your screen is recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Rememberize

Microphone access is used for voice notes.

All apps

File access is limited to the files and folders you open or select.

Purchases

If you buy through the Mac App Store, Apple handles the purchase under its own privacy policy, and RMJ4PLAY receives only sales reports containing no personal information about you. If an app is sold directly, the payment provider acts as the seller and handles payment data under its own policy.

This website

This site is static and loads no fonts, scripts, or trackers from third parties. No cookies are set. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub processes server log data such as IP addresses in order to deliver the site.

Support email

If you write in, your message and address are used to answer you and for nothing else. Legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to information, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, and to object, as well as the right to complain to a supervisory authority. Since the apps collect no data, in practice this concerns only email correspondence.
