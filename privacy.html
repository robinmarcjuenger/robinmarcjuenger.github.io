Privacy

Applies to Loopetize, Pocketize: PDF Compressor, Pocketize: Video Compressor, Rememberize, and this website. Last updated 25 October 2026.

The short version. Your files never leave your computer. There is no account, no analytics, and no tracking. The only time an app reaches the network at all is described below.

What the apps collect about you

Nothing. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking, no advertising, and no user account. Your documents, audio, video, and notes are never uploaded, and no server belonging to RMJ4PLAY receives anything from the apps.

Where your files live

All processing happens locally on your device. Files are read from and written to locations you choose. Rememberize stores notes as plain Markdown files in a folder you pick, so they stay readable without the app. Notes you choose to encrypt are saved as .md.enc files, secured with AES-256 and your password. The password and your recovery key are never sent anywhere.

When an app uses the network

Rememberize transcribes voice notes on your device. To do that it downloads its speech recognition model once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), where the model is published, the first time you use transcription. As with any download, Hugging Face receives your IP address. The download contains no other information about you, and your recordings are never sent anywhere. After that first download, transcription runs entirely on your Mac and needs no internet.

The other apps do not use the network at all.

System permissions

Loopetize

Microphone access is used to record audio into your project. Screen recording permission is used only to capture system audio, because that is the mechanism macOS provides for it. No image of your screen is recorded, stored, or transmitted. On Windows, microphone access follows the Windows privacy settings, and system audio is captured through the standard Windows audio loopback, which does not involve your screen at all.

Rememberize

Microphone access is used for voice notes.

All apps

File access is limited to the files and folders you open or select.

Purchases

If you buy through the Mac App Store, Apple handles the purchase under its own privacy policy, and RMJ4PLAY receives only sales reports containing no personal information about you. Loopetize is sold through itch.io, which handles the payment and your purchase data under its own privacy policy. Depending on your itch.io settings, RMJ4PLAY may see the name or email address attached to your purchase. It is used only to answer you if you get in touch.

This website

This site is static and loads no fonts, scripts, or trackers from third parties. No cookies are set. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub processes server log data such as IP addresses in order to deliver the site.

Support email

If you write in, your message and address are used to answer you and for nothing else. Legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to information, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, and to object, as well as the right to complain to a supervisory authority. Since the apps collect no data, in practice this concerns only email correspondence.
