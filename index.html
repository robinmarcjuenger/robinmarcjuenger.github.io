Small Mac apps that do one thing well

Every app here works on your own machine. No account, no subscription. You buy it once and it is yours.

Loopetize In development

An audio editor for game audio. Three lanes, crossfades, effects, and loop points that travel inside the file, so RPG Maker, Unity, and friends play them the way you set them.

Pocketize: PDF Compressor Coming soon

Compress PDFs and images to the size you need. Drag a target, see the result, keep the text sharp.

Pocketize: Video Compressor

The same idea for video. Pick a preset and get a much smaller file that still looks great, without learning a codec. Works on whole batches, and a video never comes out larger than it went in.

Rememberize Coming soon

A capture log for notes. Plain Markdown files in a folder you choose, so your notes outlive the app.
